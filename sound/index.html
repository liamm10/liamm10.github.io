Love for production & vinyl.

I went to music production school at eighteen and have been DJing around Calgary and Western Canada for over a decade. It's on the site on purpose, for reasons I get into at the bottom of the page.

What it sounds like.

Metro Syndicate is minimal and late-night house with a warehouse edge.

Stripped-back grooves, understated percussion, and a lot of repetition borrowed from early European house and minimal. It's built for rooms that want depth rather than drops.

Both jobs come down to sequencing.

A three-hour set and a complex workflow have the same problem: what you show first, what you hold back, how long you let something build before you resolve it, and how much you can cut before it stops making sense. The beginner move in both is putting everything good up front.
