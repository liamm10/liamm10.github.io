Product designer, based in Calgary.

Most recently I worked on CPQ and the proposal editor at Proposify, and before that spent two years at Ownly on how people buy new homes online. Right now I'm freelancing with clients.

I start with structure, not screens. In complex B2B tools the hard part is deciding what each thing is and how it connects to everything else, and the screens follow from that. I also care about visual polish, which in B2B is usually the first thing cut. The work I enjoy most is where nobody has defined the problem yet.
